Module 04

Calendars

Six ways to ask for a date. The native inputs take a value string and are done in one line. The custom pickers need you to navigate months, dodge disabled cells, and in one case work out which "15" you're actually clicking.

§ 01

Native date inputs

These accept fill('2026-11-19') directly — no clicking required. Note the formats differ: week wants 2026-W47 and datetime-local wants a T separator.

§ 02

Popup picker on a read-only field

The text input rejects typing, so the only way in is the grid. Days carry data-date, which makes this the friendly version — cells from the neighbouring months are rendered too, and clicking one jumps the view.

Delivery date
Drill-down picker no data-date

Click the month title to reach months, click again for years. Day cells expose only their number, and leading/trailing cells show duplicate numbers — filter by [data-outside="false"] or use exact text plus position.

§ 03

Date range, two panels

First click sets the start, second sets the end, third starts over. The right panel is always one month ahead, so a range crossing a month boundary needs clicks in two different grids.

§ 04

Blocked dates & bounded range

Weekends, three public holidays and everything outside a 90-day window are disabled. Clicking a disabled cell does nothing — click() succeeds and the value stays empty, which is the failure mode worth learning to catch.

Site visit (weekdays only)
Inline calendar with events

Always visible, no trigger. Dates with a marker have a scheduled job; click one to read it.

§ 05

Picker inside a frame

Same widget, one frameLocator away. The frame posts the chosen date back to this page, so you can assert on either side.
