Module 03

Accordions

Six disclosure patterns that all look identical and behave nothing alike. One keeps its panel in the DOM, one removes it, one animates for 320 ms while reporting itself visible, and one hides a form four containers deep.

§ 01

Native disclosure

<details> exposes an open attribute and no aria-expanded. Content stays in the DOM but is not visible when closed. Click the <summary>, not the details element.

§ 02

ARIA disclosure — panel removed vs hidden

Left column keeps panels in the DOM with the hidden attribute. Right column deletes the panel node on close, so toBeHidden() passes on one and toHaveCount(0) is what you need on the other.

§ 03

Animated, exclusive group 320 ms

Only one section can be open. Opening a second one closes the first, and both animate. During the transition the panel is technically in the layout but clipped to zero height — Playwright's actionability check will wait, but a bare textContent read will not.

§ 04

Lazy panels fetch on first open

Content does not exist until the header is clicked, and takes ~1.1 s to arrive. Reopening is instant because the result is cached.

§ 05

Split header text text node fragmentation

These headers read as plain sentences on screen but each word is its own <span> with whitespace between. Exact text matching fails; Playwright's normalised getByText handles it.
