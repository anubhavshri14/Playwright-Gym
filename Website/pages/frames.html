Module 06

Nested iframes

The deepest control on this page is four documents down. Getting to it means chaining four frameLocator calls, and the frames in between are named to mislead: two siblings share a name, one has no attributes at all, and one is created after the page has already loaded.

The path you're looking for
page └─ iframe#depth-1 name="shellFrame" src=level-1.html └─ iframe#depth-2 name="innerFrame" src=level-2.html └─ iframe (no name, no id) src=level-3.html └─ iframe#depth-4 name="innerFrame" src=level-4.html └─ input[data-testid="deep-input"] └─ #shadow-root → button[data-testid="deep-confirm"]

Levels 2 and 4 both answer to innerFrame. Selecting by name from the top will find the wrong one — chain by position or by src instead.
